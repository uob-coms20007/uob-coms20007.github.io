<div class="defn" markdown="1">
A __Context Free Grammar (CFG)__ consists of four components:
* An alphabet of __terminal__ symbols.
* A finite, non-empty set of __non-terminal__ symbols, disjoint from the terminals.
* A finite set of __production rules__, each of shape $X \Coloneqq \alpha$.
* A designated non-terminal called the _start symbol_, which we will usually write as $S$.
</div>

{: .defn }
A __sentential form__ is just a sequence of terminals  and nonterminals.
